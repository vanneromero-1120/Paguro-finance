# Paguro Finance - Business Rules Specification

## 1. Inventory & Stock Rules

1. **Movement-Based Derivation**:
   There is **no** manually editable `stock` field on the product entity. All product quantities are strictly derived by summing historical movements in `inventory_movements`:
   $$
   \text{current\_stock} = \sum_{\text{movements}} \text{quantity\_delta}
   $$
2. **Movement Classification & Delta Signs**:
   | Movement Type | Sign | Description |
   | :--- | :---: | :--- |
   | `PURCHASE` | `+` | Inbound receipt from a supplier purchase document. |
   | `SALE` | `-` | Outbound dispatch resulting from an issued sales invoice. |
   | `RETURN_IN` | `+` | Customer returns product into inventory. |
   | `RETURN_OUT` | `-` | Return of defective or unwanted inventory to supplier. |
   | `ADJUSTMENT_IN` | `+` | Physical inventory count adjustment (excess found). |
   | `ADJUSTMENT_OUT` | `-` | Physical inventory count adjustment (shortage found). |
   | `DAMAGED` | `-` | Damaged, expired, or unsellable stock written off. |
   | `TRANSFER_IN` | `+` | Inbound transfer between warehouses or locations. |
   | `TRANSFER_OUT`| `-` | Outbound transfer between warehouses or locations. |

3. **Negative Stock Policy**:
   - By default, inventory movements that would result in $\text{current\_stock} < 0$ for a physical product are rejected.
   - Services (`product_type = 'service'`) do not generate inventory movements and are exempt from stock constraints.

4. **Low Stock Alert**:
   A product is flagged as "Low Stock" whenever:
   $$
   \text{current\_stock} \le \text{stock\_minimum}
   $$

---

## 2. Customer & Supplier Rules

1. **Tax Identification Uniqueness**:
   Within a given company, `tax_id` (NIT, CC, RUT) must be strictly unique per customer and per supplier (`UNIQUE(company_id, tax_id)`).
2. **Payment Terms & Due Dates**:
   - Default payment terms are 30 days unless specified otherwise.
   - When drafting an invoice, $\text{due\_date} = \text{issue\_date} + \text{payment\_terms\_days}$.
   - Overdue status triggers automatically when $\text{current\_date} > \text{due\_date}$ and $\text{balance\_due} > 0$.

---

## 3. Document Numbering Sequences

1. **Sequence Format**:
   - Sales Invoices: `FAC-[YYYY]-[00001]` (configurable prefix and zero-padded sequence).
   - Expenses: Supplier invoice number recorded verbatim, plus internal sequence `GAS-[YYYY]-[00001]`.
2. **Server-Side Sequence Generation**:
   Invoice numbers must never be generated by client-side counters to prevent collisions or numbering gaps in concurrent sessions. A transactional sequence or row-level locked numbering table generates the next sequential number during invoice issuance.
